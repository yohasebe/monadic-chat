# Language Learning Apps

Apps for practicing, translating, and interpreting languages, with speech synthesis and voice input for conversation practice.

## Language Practice

![Language Practice app icon](../assets/icons/language-practice.png ':size=40')

Practice a language in a conversation that the assistant starts. The assistant's replies are read aloud. To answer by voice, press the Right Arrow key or click the voice input button to start speaking, and click the button again to finish; your speech is then sent.

Language Practice supports the providers indicated in the [availability table](../basic-usage/basic-apps.md#app-availability). For the speech settings, see [Speech Panel](../basic-usage/web-interface.md#speech-settings-panel).


## Language Practice Plus

![Language Practice Plus app icon](../assets/icons/language-practice-plus.png ':size=40')

Works like Language Practice, and adds advice on your language use. The advice is collected in the side panel under Vocabulary, Grammar, and Tips.


## Translate

![Translate app icon](../assets/icons/translate.png ':size=40')

Translate text into another language. The assistant first asks for the source and target languages. To fix the translation of a phrase, write the phrase followed by the translation you want in parentheses, like `original text(desired translation)`. These pairs are kept as the session's vocabulary and used in the following translations. The languages, vocabulary, and notes are shown in the side panel under Languages, Vocabulary, and Notes.

Translate uses the Privacy Filter. It is available for the providers marked in the [availability table](../basic-usage/basic-apps.md#app-availability). How well each language is translated depends on the provider.


## Voice Interpreter

![Voice Interpreter app icon](../assets/icons/voice-chat.png ':size=40')

Translate your speech into another language and hear the translation. The assistant first asks for the target language, then translates what you say and reads only the translation aloud.

Voice Interpreter follows the provider availability shown in the [availability table](../basic-usage/basic-apps.md#app-availability). For the speech settings, see [Speech Panel](../basic-usage/web-interface.md#speech-settings-panel).
